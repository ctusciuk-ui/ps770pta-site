Our mission

The PS 770 PTA brings together parents, guardians, teachers, and staff to support the students and community of PS 770 The New American Academy. We raise funds for classroom resources and enrichment programs, organize family and community events, and give families a voice in the life of the school.

What we do

  • Fund classroom supplies, enrichment programs, and teacher support
  • Host family events that build community across the school
  • Run fundraisers, with proceeds going directly to student programs
  • Share information between families, teachers, and school leadership
  • Welcome and support new families joining the school

All parents and guardians of PS 770 students, along with teachers and staff, are welcome to join and participate.
